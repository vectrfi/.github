VectrFi lets you invest in the crypto index fund you actually want, even when nobody offers it.

Pick the assets, choose how to weight them, and set how often to rebalance. VectrFi then keeps your portfolio on target
automatically, just as an index fund would, without the manual spreadsheet updates.

Your crypto stays in your own exchange account.
